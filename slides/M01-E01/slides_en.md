# A better definition

<div class="text-4xl leading-tight max-w-4xl mx-auto text-center">
A material is matter organised across scales so that it performs under physical constraints.
</div>

# Biological function as material performance

| Biological function | Material interpretation |
|---|---|
| Protection | impact resistance, hardness, toughness |
| Movement | elasticity, actuation, damping |
| Attachment | adhesion, friction, interfacial mechanics |
| Transport | permeability, porosity, capillarity |
| Signalling | optical, acoustic, chemical response |
| Reproduction | adhesion, packaging, fracture, release |

---

# Why this matters

This translation helps us:

- read materials papers as biologists
- collaborate with engineers and materials scientists
- understand tissues, implants, gels, adhesives, scaffolds, and coatings
- ask better questions about adaptation, constraint, and failure
- connect biodiversity to physical strategies evolved over millions of years
